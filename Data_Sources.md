
# Data Sources & References

The financial analysis and valuation of Hindustan Unilever Limited
were prepared using publicly available financial information.

## 1. Screener
Used for historical financial statements, including:
- Profit & Loss
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow Statement

Website: https://www.screener.in/

## 2. Trendlyne
Used for financial information, ratios and market-related data.

Website: https://trendlyne.com/

## 3. Groww
Used for share price and market information.

Website: https://groww.in/

## 4. Hindustan Unilever Official Website
Used for:
- Company overview
- Business information
- Investor relations
- Financial disclosures

Website: https://www.hul.co.in/

## 5. Annual Reports
Used for:
- Historical financial information
- Financial statement disclosures
- Management commentary
- Notes to accounts

Refer to the relevant HUL annual reports for the reporting years used.

## Methodology

Data was collected from publicly available sources,
organised in Excel and used for financial statement analysis,
forecasting and valuation.

Note: Data and assumptions should be cross-checked
against the original publications.
